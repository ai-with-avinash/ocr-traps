<objective>
Submit the OCR-traps Medium piece to Hacker News with a title and first-comment context drop optimized for the front-page-vote-velocity window.

Purpose: HN is a single-shot distribution channel. Title quality and first-60-min vote velocity determine whether the submission ever leaves /newest. A well-framed HN post can drive 5-20k page views and surface ML-credible readers (some of whom may be cs.DL endorser candidates), but a poorly framed one dies invisibly. This plan converts the published Medium write-up into an HN submission optimized for that channel.

Output: HN submission title + first-comment text, plus the actual submission live on news.ycombinator.com with the author-context comment posted within 5 minutes.
</objective>
